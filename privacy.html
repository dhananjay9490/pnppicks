PnP Picks – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

PnP Picks ("we", "us") publishes short deal and product-review videos on YouTube and posts deals on Telegram. This policy explains what data our software ("PnP Picks Uploader") handles and how.

1. What the PnP Picks Uploader is

PnP Picks Uploader is an internal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the PnP Picks YouTube channel. It renders one short video per day from publicly available deal information and uploads that video to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and does not act on behalf of anyone other than the channel owner.

2. Data we collect

  • From YouTube API Services: only the data needed to upload videos to our own channel and to read our own channel's basic information (channel name, video IDs, upload status). We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, channel, video or viewer.
  • From viewers of our videos or Telegram channels: nothing. We do not run our own website analytics, cookies or trackers. YouTube and Telegram handle their own data under their own privacy policies.

3. How we use and store data

  • The OAuth credential that lets the uploader publish to our channel is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, is never shared, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • YouTube API data (video IDs, upload status) is kept locally only for logging and to avoid duplicate uploads. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • We do not use YouTube API data to train machine-learning or AI models.

4. Google / YouTube API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Affiliate disclosure

Links in our videos and posts are affiliate links (Amazon Associates, Flipkart affiliate programs and others). We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Prices and availability shown are correct at the time of posting and can change.

6. Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
